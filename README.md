### 🎯 [Sampling](./sampling)

Exploring how samples can be selected from a population and how **sampling distributions** help us understand sample statistics.

**Key topics:**

* 🔍 Population vs. sample
* 🎲 Sampling with replacement
* 📊 Sample means
* 📈 Sampling distributions
* 🧠 Central Limit Theorem
* 📏 Standard error

---

### 📐 [Confidence Intervals](./confidence%20intervals)

Using AQI data to understand how **confidence intervals** can estimate population parameters from sample data.

**Key topics:**

* 📊 Sample mean
* 🎯 Confidence levels
* 📏 Standard error
* 📐 Margin of error
* 🔢 95% confidence intervals
* 🧠 Statistical inference

---

### 🧪 [Hypothesis Testing](./hypothesis%20testing)

Applying hypothesis testing to real-world **Air Quality Index (AQI)** data.

The project explores questions involving:

* 🌆 Los Angeles County vs. the rest of California
* 🗽 New York vs. Ohio
* 🌲 Michigan vs. an AQI policy threshold

**Key topics:**

* 🧪 Null and alternative hypotheses
* 📊 One-sample t-tests
* 📈 Two-sample t-tests
* 🔬 Welch's t-test
* 📉 P-values
* 🎯 Statistical significance
* 🧠 Interpreting test results

---

### 🧬 [A/B Test Hypothesis Testing](./A%2FB%20test%20hypothesis-testing)

Exploring **A/B testing and hypothesis testing** to evaluate differences between groups and support data-driven analysis.

**Key topics:**

* 🧪 A/B testing
* 📊 Experimental comparison
* 🔬 Hypothesis testing
* 📉 Statistical significance
* 🧠 Data-driven decision making
